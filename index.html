Expectations for Semester 2

Improved Learning

Building on our Semester 1 foundation to achieve deeper understanding and more practical skills in technology.

Active Participation

More hands-on, project-based activities where everyone contributes ideas and collaborates on digital creations.

Enhanced Creativity

Using technology as a tool for creative expression and innovative problem-solving in various contexts.

Our Learning Commitment

Discipline and Responsibility

We commit to completing assignments on time and using class time effectively.

Active Participation

We will engage in discussions, ask questions, and contribute to group activities.

Wise Use of Technology

We promise to use digital tools responsibly, ethically, and for constructive purposes.

Collaboration and Respect

We will work together, respect different perspectives, and help each other learn.

What Will We Learn?

Digital Skills & Technology Basics

Advanced file management, online research techniques, and digital communication tools.

Computational Thinking

Problem decomposition, pattern recognition, algorithm design, and logical reasoning.

Educational Applications

Productive use of office suites, educational software, and collaborative platforms.

ICT Mini Projects

Creating digital presentations, basic websites, simple animations, and multimedia content.
